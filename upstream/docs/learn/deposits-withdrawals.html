Deposits and withdrawals

How money gets into and out of your Papertrade balance.

Depositing

Click Deposit, pick where your funds are, and confirm in your wallet. You can deposit USDC from Hyperliquid, HyperEVM, Ethereum, Arbitrum or Solana, or USDG from Robinhood Chain. The app sends it to a deposit address that belongs only to you, and it's credited to your trading balance once it lands: within seconds from Hyperliquid, or once the bridge completes from another chain.

At least 10 USDC has to arrive after fees. A first deposit also pays a one-time 1 USDC account activation fee, and deposits from other chains pay the bridge's fee, so the app shows the least each route needs to send.

Withdrawing

Withdraw any time, up to your available balance: anything not committed as margin to an open position. The minimum is 10 USDC. Sign with your wallet and the funds go to your wallet address on Hyperliquid, or on to HyperEVM, Ethereum, Arbitrum or Solana if you choose. The app shows any route fee before you sign.
