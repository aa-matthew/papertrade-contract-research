Builder codes

A planned 1% revenue share for frontends that originate trades. Not yet live.

Coming soon

Builder codes are not yet live. This page describes the planned mechanism so integrators can see the shape of what's coming.

Builder codes will let any frontend earn a share of revenue from trades it originates. The model follows Hyperliquid's native builder-code design: a frontend tags its intents with a builder identifier, and when the trade settles the protocol routes 1% of the LP-revenue carve to the builder's address.

No impact on the user

Builder codes do not change anything the user pays. The 1% comes from the LP-revenue carve, the same source that funds staker dividends. There is no additional fee on top of the user's trade. From the user's perspective, builder codes are invisible.

Who can register

Anyone running a frontend that drives volume to Papertrade, once builder codes launch.

papertrade.xyz is the first canonical frontend

papertrade.xyz is the canonical implementation operated by the protocol team, and the protocol's relayer submits every trade.

Permissionless builder codes are coming soon. Once the on-chain plumbing is finalised, any frontend will be able to register, tag its intents with a builder identifier, and earn the 1% revenue share on the volume it brings in.

When

No firm date. We will document the registration flow, the per-intent tagging surface, and the builder-payout claim mechanics here once the on-chain plumbing is finalised.
