PAPER mint curve

The flat-then-ratchet curve that turns realised loss basis into PAPER supply. Early activity mints at a constant rate while tracked LP is underwater or small; later activity mints asymptotically less.

The shape

The curve has two regions, parametrised by four constants in PaperTokenomics:

  • Flat region (when trackedLpUsd < lpFlatThresholdUsd, including negative): mint rate is the constant flatRate = 100 PAPER per $1 of mint basis. The mint basis is the user's eligible realised loss, not necessarily the amount credited to trackedLpUsd.
  • Ratchet region (when trackedLpUsd ≥ lpFlatThresholdUsd): rate decays as the high-water-mark of cumulative LP gain past the threshold (tailProgressUsd) grows. The closed-form rate at HWM H is flatRate · (S / (S + H))² where S = tailDecayScaleUsd.

Live defaults: lpFlatThresholdUsd = $2M, flatRate = 100, tailDecayScaleUsd = $120M.

One-way ratchet (v2)

tailProgressUsd is a high-water-mark that never decreases: staker drains debit trackedLpUsd but do not reduce tailProgressUsd. Once the cumulative tail-region gain has advanced, the rate is permanently decayed by that amount. Concrete consequence: cumulative tail-region losses of $100M ⇒ HWM stays at $100M ⇒ the rate is now 100 · (120/220)² ≈ 30 PAPER/$ regardless of how often the LP got drained to stakers in between.

Early traders mint at materially higher rates than late traders. By design.

What counts as "loss"

For a close, the mint basis is the user's eligible realised loss after any LP-side loss-fee carve; a liquidation always mints on the full lost margin. On a USD-funded losing close while the queue is empty, the 2% loss-branch fee is carved from LP's side, so PAPER mints on rawLoss − fee and tracked LP advances by that same amount. On a USD-funded losing close while the queue is active, no fee is taken and the loss is earmarked in sideBucket; PAPER mints on the full loss and trackedLpUsd advances by the same amount. sideBucket only decides that this cash funds queued payouts first; neither harvest nor the release of any residual changes trackedLpUsd. Liquidations mint on the lost margin in full, regardless of queue state; tracked LP advances by that margin net of the 2% fee when one is charged. Debt-funded losing closes mint on the destroyed portion of the user's queue claim, net of the loss fee when one is charged, and tracked LP advances by the same amount. A debt-funded liquidation destroys the whole claim, charges no fee, and mints on all of it.
