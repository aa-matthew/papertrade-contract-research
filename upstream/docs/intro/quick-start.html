Quick start

What you need

  • An EVM wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Frame, Rainbow, or anything that speaks WalletConnect.
  • At least 10 USDC to fund your trading balance, on Hyperliquid, HyperEVM, Ethereum, Arbitrum or Solana, or USDG on Robinhood Chain.

1. Connect

Go to papertrade.xyz and click Connect. Your wallet pops once for a signature that registers a session key: a scoped keypair that signs your trades on your behalf for a window of time. After that single signature you won't see another wallet popup for normal trading.

2. Deposit

Click Deposit and pick where your funds are. The app sends them from your wallet to your unique deposit address: yours alone, deterministic, no one else's USDC will ever land there. A transfer from Hyperliquid is credited to your Papertrade trading balance within seconds; a deposit from another chain also waits for its bridge. At least 10 USDC has to arrive after fees; the app shows the least each route needs to send. See Deposits and withdrawals for more.

3. Trade

Pick a market, a direction (long or short), how much margin to commit, and a leverage up to 1000×. Click Buy or Sell. The protocol enters you immediately at Hyperliquid's BBO mid. Close any time; the protocol reads the current mid and settles your PnL against the LP. There is no gas fee on the user side and no popup per trade; the relayer covers gas and the session key signs.
