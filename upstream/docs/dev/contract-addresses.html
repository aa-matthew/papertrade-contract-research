Contract addresses

Papertrade's contracts on HyperEVM mainnet (chain 999).

Each address links to HyperEVMScan. The Papertrade contracts are proxies; the addresses below are the ones to call.

  • Exchange: balances, positions, settlement and the payout queue.
    0x6cd5661646289FB6E65EA5C032310fDEd797D0a2
  • BatchExecutor: submits batches of signed user intents to the Exchange.
    0x233Ff29b239abc1D7748605dA9b205C3e008626E
  • PriceOracle: reads Hyperliquid's BBO for execution prices.
    0xa9B4c7b7Ca5835652d81E25739c870D49F39cC2e
  • SessionKeyManager: registers, checks and revokes session keys.
    0x8326Eb8896e358717f10502Ec73CE8D28E37B108
  • DepositProxyFactory: derives and deploys each user's deposit address.
    0x1629b7D46bef6E13FF754174FC9F32dEa309b38F
  • PaperToken: the PAPER token.
    0xe40f17915DAa230324030003A197cdAEf2261C0e
  • PaperStaking: PAPER staking and USDC rewards.
    0xAAd6c7b0CC3014FC80FfEDAe0Ed7Ce5967b0F016
  • PaperTokenomics: the PAPER mint curve and staker LP excess.
    0x75174549dbBD44497D31a47E2E2D7406408d63D9

Collateral and governance

  • USDC: the collateral token, native Circle USDC on HyperEVM.
    0xb88339CB7199b77E23DB6E890353E22632Ba630f
  • Timelock: owns the contracts; every owner action waits 7 days here before it can run.
    0xB915Fa41e824D6aA31BB833Ee81220C0F2c8EA0F
  • Multisig: a 2-of-3 multisig that owns the Timelock; only it can schedule, execute or cancel an owner action.
    0x696292762fD5AC3677a45EdBe54EAECE4194d1F6
